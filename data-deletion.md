# Skyris — Data Deletion

Skyris stores your data (tracked flight, GPS fixes, pressure history, flight logbook,
settings) **only on your own devices**: your watch, and your phone if you use Skyris there.
Ending a flight deletes its GPS fixes and pressure history, on both devices if they're
paired; a short summary stays in each device's logbook. To delete everything, including the
logbook, uninstall the app from each device — nothing remains anywhere else.

The only data that leaves your devices is an **anonymous, random per-install identifier**
sent with flight-number lookups for rate limiting. It is not linked to you, and its
usage counters expire automatically within 24 hours. Uninstalling the app permanently
discards the identifier. (On Android phones, Google's on-device barcode reader may send
Google its own anonymous performance metrics; these contain none of your data.)

For any data request, contact **helloizzylogic@gmail.com**.
