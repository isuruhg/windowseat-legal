# Windowseat — Data Deletion

Windowseat stores your data (tracked flight, GPS fixes, pressure history, settings)
**only on your watch**. To delete it: end the flight in the app, or uninstall the app —
nothing remains anywhere else.

The only data that leaves the device is an **anonymous, random per-install identifier**
sent with flight-number lookups for rate limiting. It is not linked to you, and its
usage counters expire automatically within 24 hours. Uninstalling the app permanently
discards the identifier.

For any data request, contact **isuruhg@gmail.com**.
