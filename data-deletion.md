# Windowseat — Data Deletion

Windowseat stores your data (tracked flight, GPS fixes, pressure history, flight logbook,
settings) **only on your watch**. Ending a flight deletes its GPS fixes and pressure history;
a short summary stays in the logbook. To delete everything, including the logbook, uninstall
the app — nothing remains anywhere else.

The only data that leaves the device is an **anonymous, random per-install identifier**
sent with flight-number lookups for rate limiting. It is not linked to you, and its
usage counters expire automatically within 24 hours. Uninstalling the app permanently
discards the identifier.

For any data request, contact **helloizzylogic@gmail.com**.
